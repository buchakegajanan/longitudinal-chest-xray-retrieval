How it works

  1. 1
    Upload a chest radiograph

    Select a JPEG or PNG chest X-ray image from your device.

  2. 2
    Visual feature extraction

    ResNet-50 extracts a 2048-dimensional visual embedding from the image. FAISS finds the nearest neighbours in the dataset index.

  3. 3
    Review similar cases

    Browse visually similar historical cases, inspect follow-up examinations, and compare images side by side.

Important — Research Use Only

  • This platform is a research and clinical decision-support prototype.
  • It does not diagnose diseases or recommend treatments.
  • Use only appropriately de-identified research images.
  • Similarity is visual feature distance, not a clinical diagnosis.
